A loft for travellers who pack light.

An open, light-filled space on the first floor of a historic palazzo, fully renovated. The large window frames the side of the Cathedral like a painting: the stone changes colour as the day passes, and at sunset the golden light pours into the room.

Designed for couples or solo travellers who want the best of the old town without giving up modern comfort: a comfortable bed, premium linen, a walk-in shower, and all the little details that make the difference.

"A small room, but one of those you remember for years."
