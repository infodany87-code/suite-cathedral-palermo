The largest stay, for those who want to take their time in Palermo.

The Suite Deluxe occupies an entire historic apartment, with a separate double bedroom, a spacious living room with sofa bed, and a fully equipped kitchen, plus a private terrace overlooking the city. Ideal for families, small groups of friends, or couples who want more space and privacy.

High ceilings, original Sicilian cement tile floors, windows facing the Cathedral and furnishings that blend vintage pieces with contemporary design. Every detail is considered, from mattress quality to linen sheets.

"More than a hotel room, less of a commitment than a house. It's perfect."
